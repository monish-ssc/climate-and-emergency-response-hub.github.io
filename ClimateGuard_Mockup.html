      Situation briefing, BC Interior

      Day 3, 14:00. Written from scored data only. Previous briefing: Day 2, 14:00.

      What is happening

      Wildfire K-2041 is out of control at 4,200 ha, about 60% larger than on Day 2. Winds from the southwest at 25 km/h are pushing it northeast. A smoke advisory covers the area downwind, and the river near Community E is at its 92nd percentile for this date. CWFISECCC GeoMetWater Survey

      Statistics CanadaODHF

      What changed since Day 2

      • Community B rose from High (64) to Extreme (86) as the fire front closed from 8 km to 3 km.
      • Community A entered the High band because of the new smoke advisory.
      • People in communities above the threshold rose from 4,800 to 17,200.

      Recommended preparedness actions

      1. Open the reception centre at the Community C arena (1,200 spaces).Why: estimated shelter need of 3,440 is above current capacity.
      2. Alert care homes in Community B to prepare residents for possible relocation.Why: score 86, with the fire downwind and closing.
      3. Pre-position medical support in Community D.Why: no hospital within 50 km and an older population.
      4. Find 1,640 more shelter spaces outside the smoke area.Why: the shelter gap shown on the Situation screen.
